# Museum Understanding

Develop a deep, open-ended understanding of this museum as a whole. Research freely within the confirmed scope and follow what is genuinely distinctive, important or surprising about this particular institution. This is upstream guidance for discovery, selection and structure, not a candidate list, rating, route or visitor article.

Write only `museum-understanding.md`. Its form is deliberately free: use whatever structure best expresses the understanding rather than filling a prescribed template. Ground important factual claims with direct source links, distinguish evidence from interpretation, preserve meaningful uncertainty, and pursue museum-specific connections far enough that downstream stages will not approach the collection without knowing what kind of museum this is.
